Scan to vote

1 · Pick one (FPTP)

First Past The Post: choose your single favorite. Whoever gets the most votes wins, even without a majority. Learn more.

2 · Rank your choices (Ranked Choice)

Drag the snacks by the ⠿ handle (or use the arrows) so your favorite is at the top and your least favorite is at the bottom. Learn more.

3 · Allocate your 15 points (Score Voting)

You have 15 points to split however you like: put them all on one favorite, or spread them around. Tap + and − or type a number. The snack with the most points overall wins. Learn more.

4 · Pick every snack you approve of (Approval Voting)

Tap as many snacks as you like, or just one. Each tap means "Yes, I'd be happy with this." The snack approved by the most students wins. Learn more.

First Past The Post

Most first-choice votes wins.

Ranked Choice (instant runoff)

If nobody has over 50%, the last-place snack is eliminated and its voters' ballots move to their next choice.

Score Voting

Every student had 15 points to spend. Most total points wins.

Approval Voting

Each student approved as many snacks as they liked. The snack approved by the most students wins.

Reset all votes?

This permanently deletes every vote cast so far and lets everyone vote again. It can't be undone.
